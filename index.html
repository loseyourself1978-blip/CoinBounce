CoinBounce Support

An arcade coin pusher with a slot machine, fruit collection and a boss battle.

Drop coins from the moving slider, bounce them off pegs, light up all seven fruits and hit the BONUS target to spin the slot machine. Push coins off the edge of the pusher to win them back.

FAQ

Are the coins worth real money?

No. Coins are virtual. They have no real-world value and can’t be cashed out, exchanged or transferred. The App has no in-app purchases.

I ran out of coins. How do I get more?

When you have fewer than 10 coins, a green “Watch ad · Get 50 coins” button appears next to the coin button. Tap it. You can do this up to 5 times a day; the count resets at midnight local time. Coins are added only after the ad finishes.

How do I turn off sound, music or vibration?

Tap the gear button at the top right to open Settings, then use the Sound Effects, Music and Vibration switches. The App also stays silent when your iPhone’s mute switch is on.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the App?

Yes. Progress is stored only on your device, so uninstalling the App deletes it.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Tracking can be changed in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you can also use Settings → Ad Privacy Options in the App.

常见问题

金币值钱吗？

不值。金币是虚拟的，没有现实价值，不能提现、兑换或转让。本 App 没有内购。

金币用完了怎么办？

金币少于 10 枚时，投币按钮旁会出现绿色的“看广告 · 领 50 金币”按钮，点它即可。每天最多 5 次，按本地时间零点重置。只有广告看完才会发放金币。

怎样关闭音效、音乐或震动？

点右上角的齿轮打开“设置”，关闭对应开关即可。iPhone 静音键打开时，本 App 也不会出声。

删除 App 会丢失进度吗？

会。进度只保存在你的设备上，卸载即删除。

怎样修改广告隐私选择？

可在 iOS“设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 跟踪”中修改。欧洲经济区、英国和瑞士用户还可以在本 App 的“设置 → 广告隐私选项”中修改。
