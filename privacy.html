CoinBounce Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

CoinBounce (“the App”) is an iOS game made by an individual developer. This policy explains what information the App handles and why. The App has no accounts and no servers of its own.

1. Information we do not collect

You never need to register or sign in. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location.

2. Data stored on your device

Your game progress (coins, fruit progress, today’s rewarded-ad count) and your settings (sound, music, haptics) are stored only on your device, in the app’s local storage (UserDefaults). They are never uploaded to us or anyone else.

3. Advertising by Google AdMob

The App shows optional rewarded video ads provided by Google AdMob. Watching an ad to the end earns in-game coins. To show and measure ads, Google’s software in the App may collect:

  • the device advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt;
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device information, such as model and operating system version;
  • ad interaction data, such as impressions, clicks and video views;
  • diagnostic data, such as crash and performance information.

Google processes this data under its own policies. See the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

4. Your choices

  • Tracking: you can allow or deny tracking when the App first asks. To change it later, go to iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. If you deny tracking, you may still see ads, but they won’t be personalized using the advertising identifier.
  • Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland: users in these regions manage their ad consent through Google’s consent message. You can change your choice at any time in the App under Settings → Ad privacy options (广告隐私选项).
  • Watching ads is optional. You can keep playing without watching any ads.

5. No purchases, no real-money gambling

The App has no in-app purchases and no real-money gambling. In-game coins are virtual: they have no real-world value and cannot be cashed out, exchanged or transferred.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Deleting your data

Uninstalling the App deletes all the data it stored on your device. For data held by Google, see the Google Privacy Policy linked above.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, please open an issue at GitHub Issues.

CoinBounce 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 27 日

CoinBounce（以下简称“本 App”）是一款由个人开发者制作的 iOS 游戏。本政策说明本 App 会处理哪些信息以及原因。本 App 没有账号系统，也没有自己的服务器。

1. 我们不收集的信息

无需注册或登录。我们不收集你的姓名、邮箱、电话、通讯录、照片或位置。

2. 保存在你设备上的数据

游戏进度（金币、水果收集进度、当天看激励广告的次数）和设置（音效、音乐、震动）只保存在你的设备上（App 本地存储 UserDefaults），不会上传给我们或任何人。

3. Google AdMob 广告

本 App 通过 Google AdMob 提供可选的激励视频广告，完整看完可获得游戏金币。为了展示和衡量广告，本 App 中的 Google 软件可能会收集：

  • 设备广告标识符（IDFA），仅在你通过苹果的“App 跟踪透明度”弹窗允许跟踪后；
  • IP 地址，以及由此推断的大致位置；
  • 设备信息，例如机型和系统版本；
  • 广告互动数据，例如展示、点击和视频观看；
  • 诊断数据，例如崩溃和性能信息。

这些数据由 Google 依照其政策处理，详见 Google 隐私权政策 和 Google 如何使用来自使用其服务的网站或应用的信息。

4. 你的选择

  • 跟踪：首次询问时你可以允许或拒绝。以后可在 iOS“设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 跟踪”中更改。拒绝后仍可能看到广告，但不会使用广告标识符进行个性化。
  • 欧洲经济区、英国和瑞士用户：通过 Google 的同意弹窗管理广告同意，并可随时在本 App 的“设置 → 广告隐私选项”中修改。
  • 看广告是自愿的，不看也可以继续游戏。

5. 无内购，无真实货币赌博

本 App 没有内购，也没有任何真实货币赌博。游戏金币是虚拟的，没有现实价值，不能提现、兑换或转让。

6. 儿童

本 App 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，我们也不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

7. 删除数据

卸载本 App 即会删除它在你设备上保存的全部数据。Google 持有的数据请参阅上方的 Google 隐私权政策。

8. 政策变更

如本政策有更新，我们会更新本页面和上方的生效日期。

9. 联系方式

如有隐私相关问题，请在 GitHub Issues 留言。
